# Gym workspace

Mode: Operate. Audience: gym staff working through daily classes, rosters and member tasks; members booking their own training. Use the approved interactive prototype supplied as athleta-approved-prototype.html as visual authority. No further direction selection is needed.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A compact, calm operations desk for the gym's daily rhythm, not a marketing landing page or decorative analytics dashboard.

OWN-WORLD: Forest navigation anchors chalk working surfaces; court-lime marks the live training panel; green check-in actions remain unmistakable. Sentence case, quiet hairlines and native form controls keep tasks legible.

STORY: Staff scan the day's classes, open a roster, and complete attendance without losing context. Members have separate navigation for timetable, bookings and profile.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 210px forest sidebar, 60px white topbar, 28px content inset and compact four-column summary precede a wide class list and 248px live rail. Mobile reflows rows and moves navigation to the bottom. Signature interaction: check-in updates the roster and can be undone.

FORM: seed: none (pinned). “Approved compact calm operations prototype” pins the direction and composition.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Unslop direction

- Product: Gym staff manage training and members; members manage their own bookings.
- Character: Calm, exact, welcoming.
- World: A gym operations desk, with chalk surfaces and forest-green navigation.
- Moments: The live class and its real check-in count carry court-lime; attendance actions carry green.
- Signature: A restrained live dot and reversible check-in action, never decorative animation.
